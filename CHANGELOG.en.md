# What's new in Chat Reactions

One section per version, newest first.

## 1.3.1 — 2026-11-21

- With the dnd5e 5 system, the reactions under a message now respond to the mouse: you can see who reacted, a click adds or takes back a reaction, and the reply counter takes you to the replies. The message background drawn by that system was covering them.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-16

- **Replies.** An arrow in the message header lets you reply to it with a quote, the way social networks do. The reply shows who and what it answers; clicking the quote takes you to the original message, even one far up the chat. Under the original a reply counter takes you through the replies one by one.
- A reply to a whisper goes as a whisper to the same people. The quote of a whisper is visible only to those who saw the whisper itself.
- Replies can be switched off for the whole world in the module settings.
- The placeholder of a removed custom icon now shows as a picture icon, not an empty square.
- Changing custom icons no longer pulls older, not yet loaded messages into the chat.

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-16

- Taking back your last reaction no longer leaves an empty entry for you in the message data.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-16

- **Custom icons.** The GM adds their own images to the reaction set, next to the emoji: a party crest, the innkeeper's face, a mug of ale. Module settings → "Custom icons" → "Edit icons": an image path or the file picker, and a label. The label shows on hover.
- A removed icon stays under messages as a placeholder and can be taken back like any other. You can swap an icon's image for another file — reactions already given stay.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-11-16

- Clicking the smiley again closes the emoji picker instead of reopening it.
- The module archive now follows the zip standard, so it installs on Linux servers too.
- Screenshots and the "Module language" setting in the README.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-16

- **Favourites.** A star in the message header saves it to your journal "Favourites: your name": when it was said and what; for a roll, the formula and the total. Only you and the GM can see the journal.
- **Page arrangement is everyone's own choice:** a page for each speaker (the default), for each participant or for each message. On a shared page entries run in the order the messages were sent.
- An entry is a copy: a deleted message stays in the journal. Clicking the star again removes only that entry; a page left empty disappears.
- Saved messages show a gold star; "Add to favourites" and "Remove from favourites" are also in the right-click menu.
- A whisper can be saved only by those it was addressed to, a blind roll only by the GM.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-31

- Emoji reactions under any chat message: a smiley in the header, an emoji picker, the count and names on hover.
- Reactions to other people's messages are recorded by the GM; nobody can react in someone else's name.
- The emoji set can be changed in the module settings.
