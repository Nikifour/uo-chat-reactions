# UO · Chat Reactions

## How to react

Hover over a chat message: a smiley appears in its header, next to the time. Click it and choose an emoji. The emoji appears under the message with a number: how many people gave it.

Click an emoji under a message to add the same one or take yours back. Your own reactions are highlighted in gold. Hover over an emoji to see who gave it.

You can give several emoji to one message. Anything in the chat can get reactions: lines, rolls, spell and attack cards.

## Who records reactions

A player changes their own messages directly. Foundry does not let players change other people's messages, so the GM's client records those reactions — silently, in a fraction of a second. With no GM in the game you can only react to your own messages.

Reactions are stored in the message itself: everyone who sees the message sees them, and they survive a reload. Delete the message and its reactions go with it.

## Favourites

Next to the smiley in a message header there is a star. Click it and the message goes into your journal **"Favourites: your name"** in the "Favourites" folder: when it was said and what; for a roll, the formula and the total.

How entries are arranged into pages is everyone's own choice, in the module settings under "Favourites: pages":

- **a page for each speaker** (the default) — everything Mira said on the page "Mira", everything the innkeeper said on the page "Innkeeper". A hero is recognised by their sheet, so a renamed token does not split the page in two;
- **a page for each participant** — by the person at the keyboard: everything from one player or GM, with all of their characters, on one page, and every entry says who was speaking;
- **a page for each message** — a separate page for every one.

On a shared page entries run in the order the messages were sent, not the order they were saved. Change the arrangement and new entries follow it; what is already saved is not rearranged.

The journal is yours: you and the GM can see it, everyone else cannot. It is a copy, not a link — if the message is deleted or the chat is cleared, the entry stays. You can edit the pages and add to them by hand.

Saved messages show a gold star all the time, not only on hover. Click the star again to remove the message from favourites: only that entry leaves the page, and a page left empty disappears. The same is in the message's right-click menu: "Add to favourites" and "Remove from favourites".

A whisper can be saved only by those it was addressed to, and a blind roll only by the GM.

The first time, the journal is created by the GM: Foundry does not let players create journals. So the first star works while a GM is in the game; after that the journal is yours and you write to it yourself.

## Replies

Next to the smiley and the star in a message header there is an arrow. Click it and a bar appears above the chat input: "Reply to: who — what about". Write your reply and send it as usual: a quote of the message you replied to appears above yours. Changed your mind? The cross on the bar, or Esc. The same is in the message's right-click menu: "Reply".

Clicking the quote takes you to the original message: the chat scrolls to it and highlights it, loading older history if it is far up. Under the original a reply counter with an arrow grows; clicking it takes you through the replies one by one, and hovering shows who replied.

A reply to a whisper goes as a whisper to the same people: the recipients and the author of the original. If you pick the recipients yourself — with /w or the "GM only" mode — the module leaves your choice alone.

The quote of a whisper is visible only to those who saw the whisper itself; everyone else sees "hidden message". If the original is deleted, a reply to a public message keeps its quote, and a reply to a whisper says "message deleted".

Only what you type in the chat becomes a reply: a roll from a character sheet or a spell card sent while the bar is shown does not. Replies can be switched off for the whole world in the module settings; quotes already written stay.

## The emoji set

The GM changes the set in the module settings: "Reaction set", emoji separated by spaces. New emoji show up in the picker at once. Reactions already given stay under their messages even if you remove their emoji from the set, and you can always take yours back.

## Custom icons

Besides emoji you can add your own images to the set: a party crest, the innkeeper's face, a mug of ale. In the module settings — "Custom icons" → "Edit icons": the image path (or the file picker button) and a label. Any image file works — png, webp, svg, gif. The images join the picker after the emoji; the label shows on hover.

An icon removed from the list stays under the messages where it was already given, as a grey placeholder, and can be taken back like any other. You can swap an icon's image for another file without removing it: reactions already given stay.
